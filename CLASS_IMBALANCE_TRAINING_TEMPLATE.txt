SIH PS 26017 — Class Imbalance Training Template

The target is approximately imbalanced (~72% delayed / ~28% not delayed).
Do not rebalance validation or test.

Recommended starting point: Option A, tree-based weighting.
For XGBoost:
    neg = (train.target_is_delayed == 0).sum()
    pos = (train.target_is_delayed == 1).sum()
    scale_pos_weight = neg / pos

For LightGBM:
    LGBMClassifier(class_weight="balanced")

For RandomForest:
    RandomForestClassifier(class_weight="balanced")

Alternative linear baseline:
    LogisticRegression(class_weight="balanced", max_iter=1000)

Alternative resampling:
    Upsample ONLY the training split. Never resample val/test.

Evaluate with F1, precision, recall, ROC-AUC and confusion matrix;
do not rely on plain accuracy.

Drop before X:
    target_is_delayed, label_confidence_tier, is_unlabeled,
    ml_split_v2, project_id, quarter
